Accessibility has always been a core commitment I have held my nonsense to. But while color contrast and screen reader compatability are one thing, mobile compatability simply makes me angry. Smartphones are a replusive soulless and vile disgrace to technology[^1] and I loathe them with more bile than every liver on earth could ever secrete[^2]. Why should I mutilate my website to allow a *phone user* to get their dirty little screen rubbing fingers all over it‽
<br><br>
But I eventually have acquiesced and updated my website to work on phones[^3]. I did this because not all phone users are incapable of change. By withholding my knowledge from those I deam unworthy, I rob them of the ability to know better. The issue of mobile uses would only have worsened without my influence to enlighten them. 
<br><br>
Though it's still to early to tell whether I've made a significant dent in smartphones' userbase, putting aside my hatred for benevolence has made me a better person. The only way for meaningful growth is access to information[^4].
